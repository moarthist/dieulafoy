A library study · Six albums

Jane Dieulafoy The Photographs

Six leather albums lie on the table of a library study. Between them they hold every captioned page of the Dieulafoy albums kept at the INHA library in Paris, 774 in all: the road to Persia, its mosques and bridges, its ancient empires, the excavation of Susa, its people, and Spain and France.
